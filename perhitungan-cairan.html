💧 ICU Fluid & Electrolyte Calculator

🔼 Hipernatremia – Defisit Air
Referensi:
TBW = BB × (0.6 pria | 0.5 wanita)
Defisit air = TBW × [(Na/140) − 1]
Kecepatan koreksi: akut 1 mEq/L/jam, kronis ≤0.5 mEq/L/jam
🔽 Hiponatremia – Laju Koreksi
Referensi:
Ringan ≤0.5 mEq/L/jam
Sedang ≤1.0 mEq/L/jam
Berat ≤1.5 mEq/L/jam
Na <120 mEq/L → risiko edema serebri
🧪 Hipokalemia – Estimasi Defisit
Referensi:
Defisit K ≈ 0.3 × BB × ΔK
Peripheral IV ≤8 mEq/jam
Central line 10–20 mEq/jam + EKG
🦴 Kalsium Plasma
Referensi:
Normal 8.5–10.5 mg/dL
CaCl₂ 10% 3–5 mL (sentral)
Ca Glukonat 10% 10–20 mL
🧬 Fosfat Plasma
Referensi:
Normal 2.5–4.5 mg/dL
<1 mg/dL → IV fosfat (ICU)
⚡ Magnesium Plasma
Referensi:
Normal 1.7–2.4 mg/dL
Kejang → MgSO₄ 1–2 g IV
🩺 AKI – KDIGO Staging
KDIGO Criteria:
Stage 1: 1.5–1.9× baseline
Stage 2: 2.0–2.9× baseline
Stage 3: ≥3.0× baseline atau Cr ≥4 mg/dL
